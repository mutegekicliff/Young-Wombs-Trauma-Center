Our Purpose

Rising Strong With Young Mothers & Vulnerable Girls

Young Wombs Trauma Centre supports teenage mothers, survivors of sexual and gender-based violence (SGBV), out-of-school girls, and underserved young women across Uganda and East Africa.

We believe every young mother deserves the opportunity to heal, learn, grow, and build a self-sustained future for herself and her child.

Our Vision

A Nurtured & Dignified Future

“A mentally healthy, educated, self-sustained, nurtured and dignified teenage mother.”

Our Mission

Holistic Support & Action

“To mobilise healthcare interventions, inclusive education, advocacy and financial literacy for teenage mothers, supporting sustainable living.”

Our Framework

Four Core Strategic Areas

1. Healthcare

Reproductive and maternal health information, maternal care access, and emotional wellbeing.

2. Inclusive Education

Returning to formal schooling, vocational training, and technology/digital skills.

3. Advocacy

Dignity, rights protection, anti-stigma campaigns, and community participation.

4. Financial Literacy

Budgeting, savings, financial record keeping, and income-earning opportunities.

To mobilise healthcare interventions, inclusive education, advocacy and financial literacy for teenage mothers, supporting sustainable living.

Motto: "Rising Strong"
