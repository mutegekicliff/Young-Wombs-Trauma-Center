Our Approach

Holistic Support for Teenage Mothers

We combine healthcare access, emotional recovery, educational reintegration, rights advocacy, and livelihood training to build lasting self-reliance.

Core Principles

  • Participant Choice: Respecting each young mother's agency and goals.
  • Trauma-Informed: Creating safe, supportive learning and healing environments.
  • Community Engagement: Reducing social stigma through active advocacy.
Strategic Pillars

Four Core Strategic Areas

Pillar 01

Healthcare & Emotional Wellbeing

Reproductive health information, maternal health access, emotional recovery support, and maternal care guidance.

Pillar 02

Inclusive Education

Returning to formal schooling, vocational learning, technology skills, and digital empowerment.
