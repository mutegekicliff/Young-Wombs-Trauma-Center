Menstrual Health & Dignity

Ensuring No Girl Misses School Due to Her Period

The Periods With Dignity Campaign addresses menstrual health taboos, lack of educational guidance, and inadequate access to hygiene products that lead to school absenteeism among adolescent girls.

We work directly with schools, parents, and community leaders to foster open dialogue, distribute menstrual hygiene packs, and build supportive environments.

Campaign Focus Areas

Key Pillars of Action

1. Health Education

Age-appropriate menstrual health workshops covering reproductive biology, hygiene management, and self-care in schools and community groups.

2. Dignity Product Access

Providing essential sanitary product packages to vulnerable schoolgirls and young mothers to prevent period poverty from hindering learning.

3. Stigma Reduction

Community advocacy sessions involving boys, parents, and teachers to eliminate shame and promote menstrual dignity for all girls.
