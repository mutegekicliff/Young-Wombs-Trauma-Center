Proposed Community-Based Violence Prevention Programme

Developed from our Rising Strong experience in Kericho, Kenya and Wakiso, Uganda. Moving communities from awareness to accountability and from discussion to prevention.

Partner With MezaDuara
Community Roundtable Approach

The Rising Strong Community Roundtable for Violence Prevention

MezaDuara Initiative is a proposed community-based violence prevention programme developed by Young Wombs Trauma Centre from its Rising Strong experience with teenage mothers, adolescent girls, and survivors of violence.

“Meza Duara” is a Swahili term that refers to a roundtable approach. The programme brings together adolescent girls and young women, parents and caregivers, male champions, cultural and religious leaders, teachers, health workers, police, local government, child-protection actors, and other community stakeholders.

Together, these stakeholders discuss the root drivers of sexual and gender-based violence (SGBV), learn about prevention and survivor-centred responses, and develop practical community-led solutions.

Inclusive Dialogue

Bringing the Community Together

Effective violence prevention requires active participation from all levels of community leadership and influence.

Girls & Young Women

Adolescent girls, young mothers, and survivors sharing lived experiences and leading safe reflection.

Parents & Caregivers

Building supportive home environments, eliminating neglect, and protecting girls from vulnerability.

Male Champions

Engaging men and boys as allies in dismantling harmful gender norms and stopping violence.

Cultural & Religious Leaders

Transforming harmful social norms and promoting justice, dignity, and care within faith & tradition.

Teachers & Health Workers

Providing survivor-centred first care, early identification, and trauma-sensitive support in schools & clinics.

Police & Child-Protection Actors

Strengthening legal pathways, local government accountability, and referral mechanisms for justice.

Addressing Root Drivers

Comprehensive Prevention & Protection

The MezaDuara model systematically targets violence in both the family and broader community.

Defilement & SGBV

Educating communities on sexual rights, legal protections, and reporting channels to stop sexual violence against minors.

Child Marriage

Challenging forced early marriages and keeping girls in school through community watchdog networks.

Domestic Violence & Neglect

Addressing intimate partner violence and parental neglect that pushes adolescent girls into extreme vulnerability.

Harmful Social Norms

Deconstructing deep-seated cultural expectations and gender inequalities that enable violence.

Stigma & Discrimination

Eliminating shame surrounding teenage pregnancy and sexual assault to enable survivor healing.

Breaking the Silence

Creating safe spaces and practical support structures so girls feel empowered to speak up and seek help.

Implementation Model

How MezaDuara Drives Community Action

Through structured steps, MezaDuara transforms community dialogue into actionable accountability.

01

Structured Dialogues

Facilitated discussions on local SGBV drivers.

02

Safe Reflection

Trauma-informed space to reflect without judgment.

03

Referral Mapping

Mapping legal, health, and psychosocial pathways.

04

Action Planning

Co-designing community-led solutions & bylaws.

05

Follow-up Meetings

Ensuring ongoing community accountability.

Implementation Scope

Kericho, Kenya & Wakiso, Uganda

MezaDuara Initiative will be implemented by Young Wombs Trauma Centre in Kericho County, Kenya, and Wakiso District, Uganda. We welcome funding partners, child protection advocates, and community leaders to join us in bringing this roundtable model to life.
